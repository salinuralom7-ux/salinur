# MySheher — Meta ad campaign, phase one

Written for the situation as it actually is on 7 August 2026: the app is in
closed testing and cannot be installed by the public, the website works and
takes registrations, and there are almost no service experts listed yet.

Everything here follows from one decision: **advertise to service experts, not to
customers, until there are enough service experts to be worth finding.**

---

## Why service experts first

A marketplace fails from the supply side, and it fails quietly.

A customer who opens MySheher, searches "electrician", and finds nobody does
not come back. They do not complain, they do not tell you, and they are gone
for good — the first impression of an empty app is the only impression. A
service expert who registers and waits a week for a booking is mildly disappointed and
still there.

So the money goes on service experts until there are roughly **50 live profiles across
the common trades**. Then, and only then, turn it round.

Spending on customer ads before that is not just wasted — it actively burns
the audience you will want later.

---

## Do not run an app-install campaign yet

The Play listing is closed-testing only. Anyone not on the tester list sees
"Item not found."

An install campaign would:

* send people to a dead link and lose them permanently,
* teach Meta's optimiser that nobody converts, which poisons the ad account's
  learning for later,
* and spend money for nothing.

Wait until the app is in production. Then the install campaign becomes phase
three.

---

## The campaign

**One campaign, two ad sets, three ads each.** Do not build more than this at
the start; a small budget split many ways learns nothing.

### Campaign

| | |
|---|---|
| Objective | **Engagement → WhatsApp messages** (primary) |
| Second campaign | **Traffic → Website** (secondary, smaller budget) |
| Buying type | Auction |
| Special ad category | **None** — this is not employment, housing, credit or politics |
| Budget | Campaign budget optimisation ON |

**On "special ad category":** Meta restricts targeting for **employment** ads.
MySheher is not hiring anybody — it is a directory where independent service experts
list themselves, and no job is being offered by you. Declaring "employment"
would strip your location and age targeting for no reason. If Meta's review
disagrees and flags it, accept the flag rather than arguing; the campaign
still runs, just with wider targeting.

### Why WhatsApp first, website second

In Assam, a click-to-WhatsApp ad converts several times better than a click to
a web form. People are already in WhatsApp, they trust it, and they can ask a
question before committing. It also gives you a phone number and a conversation
you can follow up — a website visit that bounces gives you nothing.

The trade-off is that you have to answer those messages. If you cannot reply
within a few hours, run the website campaign instead.

## Targeting

### Ad set A — the trades

| | |
|---|---|
| Location | **Guwahati + 25 km** (drop a pin on Guwahati, set radius) |
| Age | 21–55 |
| Gender | All |
| Languages | Assamese, Hindi, Bengali, English |
| Detailed targeting | Leave **empty** at first |
| Placements | **Advantage+ (automatic)** |

Leaving interests empty is deliberate. Guwahati + 25 km is roughly 1.5 million
people — small enough that Meta's own optimisation will find the responders
faster than your guesses will. Interest targeting on top of a small geography
starves the algorithm.

### Ad set B — the same, restricted to Facebook feed only

Same targeting, placements set to **Facebook Feed only**.

This exists to answer one question you cannot answer by guessing: whether your
people are on Facebook or Instagram. In small-city Assam it is usually
Facebook by a wide margin, and knowing that reshapes everything later.

---

## Budget

Start at **₹300/day total**, split by campaign budget optimisation.

That is about ₹9,000 a month, and at Guwahati CPMs it buys enough impressions
to learn from. Do not start higher: you will spend faster than you can learn,
and you will not know which of three ads did the work.

**Run it for seven days without touching anything.** Meta needs roughly 50
conversions per ad set to leave the learning phase, and every edit resets that
clock. The urge to fiddle on day two is the single most common way small
advertisers waste money.

After seven days:

* kill any ad below half the best ad's result rate,
* double the budget on what worked,
* only then write new variants.

## Creative

**Use photographs of real Guwahati service experts.** Not stock, not illustrations, not
the app's screenshots. A face someone recognises as their own kind of person
outperforms polished design by a distance, and it is the one thing a
competitor cannot copy.

* Ask three or four service experts you know for permission to photograph them at work.
  Get the permission in writing over WhatsApp.
* Shoot on a phone, in daylight, at the job — an electrician on a ladder, a
  cook in a kitchen, a tailor at a machine.
* **1:1 square** (1080×1080) for feed. Also export **4:5** (1080×1350); it takes
  more screen and usually wins.
* Put no more than eight words on the image. Text belongs in the caption.
* Never show a service expert's phone number.

If you have no photographs yet, run text-on-brand-colour cards using the app's
own palette (#15151A background, the yellow) — but replace them with real faces
within two weeks. Prioritise this; it is the highest-leverage thing on this
page.

## Setting it up

1. **A Facebook Page must exist** — ads cannot run from a personal profile.
   Create one named **MySheher**, category *Local Service*, with the app icon
   as the profile picture and the feature graphic as the cover.
2. **business.facebook.com** → create a Business Portfolio → add the Page.
3. **Ads Manager** → create an ad account. Currency **INR**, timezone
   **Asia/Kolkata**. Neither can be changed later — get them right.
4. Add a payment method. In India that is usually a card; UPI autopay works on
   some accounts.
5. Connect the WhatsApp number **+91 70865 99367** to the Page, for the
   click-to-WhatsApp ads.
6. Build the campaign as above. Publish.
7. **Do not touch it for seven days.**

## What to measure

Ignore likes, reach and impressions. They tell you nothing.

| Metric | What good looks like |
|---|---|
| Cost per WhatsApp conversation started | under ₹40 |
| Conversations → registrations | over 30% |
| **Cost per live service expert profile** | **under ₹150** |

The last one is the only number that matters. Everything else is a step
towards it. Count it by hand each week: money spent ÷ profiles approved.

If a live profile is costing more than ₹300, stop and change the creative
before spending more. If it is under ₹100, raise the budget.

---

## Phase two and three, for later

**Phase two — customers.** Once ~50 profiles are live: same geography, ages
25–55, objective Traffic or Messages, creative showing real service experts with real
rates. Lead with the specific ("Electrician at home, ₹300 a visit, in Six
Mile"), never the generic.

**Phase three — app installs.** Only once the app is in production on Play.
Objective App Promotion, which needs the Facebook SDK or a measurement partner
in the app — a real engineering job, not a settings change. Do not promise
yourself this is quick.

---

## Two things not to do

**Do not boost posts.** The Boost button is a worse version of the campaign
above, with no targeting control and no comparison between creatives. It is
where small advertisers' money goes to die.

**Do not claim what MySheher does not do.** No "background-verified", no
"police-verified", no "guaranteed". Your review confirms a profile looks
genuine; the app says so plainly and the ads must match. A service expert who joins on
a promise you did not make is a complaint waiting to happen, and Meta pulls ads
that overstate.
