## Posting rhythm

**Week 1** — posts 1 to 6, one a day. All worker-facing. This is recruitment.
**Week 2** — posts 7 to 9, then start showing real workers who have joined.
**Week 3 onward** — two a week is enough. One worker story, one useful post.

The single most valuable thing you can post, once you have workers: **a real
worker, photographed, named, with their trade and their rate.** It recruits
other workers better than anything written, and it is the only content a
competitor cannot copy.

Ask permission every time, and get it in writing over WhatsApp. Never post a
worker's phone number.

## What not to do

* No stock photos of white people in call centres. Guwahati will notice.
* No "download now!!!" until the app is actually installable — the link fails
  and people do not come back for a second try.
* No claims of background checks or police verification. You do not do them,
  the app says so, and saying otherwise on Instagram is the sort of thing that
  ends up quoted back at you.
* No follow-for-follow, no engagement pods, no bought followers. A local
  marketplace lives on real local reach; a fake follower in Jakarta is worse
  than useless because it drags your reach down.
