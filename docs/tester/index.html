Get MySheher on your phone

MySheher is not public on the Play Store yet. Google only lets people in on an invitation list — which is why tapping a normal Play Store link shows Item not found. Three steps, in this order, and it works.

Android only, for now

The Play Store test is for Android phones. On an iPhone, skip to the last section — you can still use MySheher, just not through an app store.

Three steps

1

Check which Google account your phone uses

Do this first. It takes ten seconds and it is the reason half of these go wrong.

Open the Play Store → tap your picture at the top right → read the address under your name.

That exact address is the one that has to be on our list — not the one you email from, not the one on your WhatsApp. Most people have two or three, and the invitation only works for the one Play is signed in with.

It also has to be a Google account: a gmail.com address, or a work address that signs in with Google. An address that has never been used with Google cannot be invited at all.

If you are not certain we have that exact address, send it to us on WhatsApp before going on. Adding you takes us two minutes; guessing wastes your afternoon.

2

Join the test

Tap the button below on the Android phone you will use. Sign in if Google asks. Then press Become a tester on the page that opens.

Wait for it to say you are a tester before doing anything else. This is the step everybody misses, and skipping it is what produces Item not found.

Become a tester
3

Install it

On that same page, tap Download it on Google Play. If you have closed the page, use the button below instead.

The Play Store will open on MySheher with an Install button. If it still says the app is not available, give it ten minutes and pull down to refresh — Google is sometimes slow to notice you have joined.

Open MySheher on Google Play

Still says “Item not found”?

It is nearly always one of these five, in this order of likelihood.

You skipped step 2.

Tapping the Play Store link on its own will not work, ever, no matter how many times you try it. Go back and press Become a tester first.

Your phone is signed in to a different Google account.

Most people have two or three Google accounts, and the Play Store uses whichever is on the phone — not the one your invitation was sent to. That is what step 1 is for. If the address there is not the one we have, either switch account in the Play Store and start again, or send us the one it shows.

You are not on the list yet.

Only the email addresses we have been given can see the app. If you have not sent us the exact Google account address on your phone, send it now — WhatsApp us — and we will add you. It takes a couple of minutes on our side.

You have just been added.

Google can take a few hours to let a new tester through, occasionally longer. Nothing is wrong. Try again later the same day before telling us it is broken.

You are outside India.

MySheher is released to India only, so Play hides it everywhere else. If you are abroad right now, the app is genuinely unavailable to you until we widen it.

Please stay a tester for two weeks

This is the part that actually matters to us, and it is easy to undo by accident.

Google will only let MySheher go public once twelve people have been testers continuously for fourteen days. If you join and then leave the test — or uninstall and remove yourself — the count drops and the fourteen days start again for everyone. So: install it, keep it on your phone, and please do not press Leave the programme.

You do not have to use it every day. Just keep it installed. Opening it once in a while and telling us what is broken is a bonus, and honestly the most useful thing you could do for us.

No Play Store, or an iPhone

MySheher is a website as well as an app, and the website is the same thing — same profiles, same bookings, same everything.

Open mysheher.com in Chrome or Safari, then:

  • Android / Chrome — menu (⋮) → Add to Home screen
  • iPhone / Safari — Share → Add to Home Screen

It then behaves like any other app on your phone. Worth saying plainly: this does not count towards Google's twelve testers, so if you are on Android please do the Play Store route as well.
