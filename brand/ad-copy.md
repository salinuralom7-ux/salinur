# Nearse — launch ad pack

Written against `ad-prompt.md`. Everything here is checked against the NEVER
list: no uniforms, no "verified", no payment imagery, no invented numbers, no
claims outside Guwahati.

Two audiences, and they need opposite ads. **Workers first.** A marketplace
with no workers has nothing to sell, so for the first few weeks put most of
the spend on the worker side and let the customer ads follow.

Assamese lines are marked **[AS]**. Read them aloud before using them — the
meaning is right but a native ear should pick the phrasing.

# Before you publish

Run every asset past these five:

1. Nobody is wearing a Nearse uniform, and nothing implies workers are our
   staff.
2. The word **verified** does not appear next to a person's name or face. Say
   "checked by a person" instead.
3. No cards, no UPI screens, no cash changing hands.
4. No invented numbers — no worker counts, no download counts, no star
   ratings, no testimonials from people who do not exist.
5. Nothing claims Nearse works outside Guwahati.

The first two are legal, not stylistic. Your own terms say the review confirms
a profile looks genuine and is **not** a background check; an ad that says
"verified" contradicts the page you published.
