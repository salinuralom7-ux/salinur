# THE style (Salinur, 2026-10-08): follow ref17 100 %. All earlier references are retired.

Source: references/ref17 (a "Raw vs Edit" showcase; the edit = right panel, cropped in ref17/edit_panel.mp4).
Feel: minimal, clean, easy to understand, but expertly edited. Visual storytelling on every idea.

## Look
- Talking head stays steady and centred (medium shot), soft slow push-ins only. No aggressive jump-zooms.
- Grade: warm pastel. Lifted soft blacks, warm/peach highlights, gentle contrast, natural skin. Palette = peach/coral/cream.
- Soft dark gradient over the lower ~40 % so captions read cleanly.
- Small IG glyph + @handle watermark top-right, always.

## Captions (lower-middle, ~63-68 % of height, centred)
- 1-3 words at a time, exactly on the spoken words.
- Small words: clean bold sans, white, soft shadow.
- ONE keyword per phrase: big, heavy sans, soft peach/cream, letters appear one by one.
- Optional handwritten script word overlapping the keyword (small, white): "really", "will", "brand", "that".
- Sometimes a keyword gets a tiny graphic: numbers as giant numerals, a row of avatar circles above "people",
  letters swapped for icons, a ruler/timeline sliding under a year.

## Visual-story scenes (every 3-6 s, 1.5-4 s long, full screen)
1. Hook: video inside a rounded card on a peach background, 3D emoji faces floating around -> card expands to full screen.
2. Timeline/ruler that slides under a number (2021 2022 ... 2025 big peach).
3. Hero cut-out on coral chevron background + huge 3D-shadow word + script word.
4. Dark charcoal grid + white circle "spotlight" that swings to icons one by one (calendar, storm, globe).
5. Plain peach gradient + handwritten sentence writing itself on, red underline under the key word, then a ring wipe.
6. Peach background + coral wave line; rounded photo cards with labels slide in one after another (carousel).
7. Outro: everything dims, IG glyph white -> gradient, @handle, ~3-4 s.

## Transitions
- Light-leak flashes (green glow, orange flare) of ~0.2-0.3 s back into the talking head.
- Quick blur-dissolve, circular ring wipe, card slide. Never plain hard cuts on graphics.
- A small white pill/dot drifting around the caption = constant subtle motion.

## Sound
- Soft modern instrumental bed, whoosh on every light leak / scene change, soft pops on cards/icons. Voice always on top.
