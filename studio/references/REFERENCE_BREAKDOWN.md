## Combined style to apply to Salinur's videos

### The one big rule
**The speaker stays on screen almost all the time.** Graphics are layered *behind* him (person cut-out in front), in the upper half, or as small cards — never a static full-screen card while he talks. Full-screen cutaways are rare and short (< 1.5 s) and always moving.

### Captions (Salinur said v1/v2 captions were low quality)
| Setting | Value | From |
|---|---|---|
| Base caption | Clean white sans, **semibold/bold, no outline, no box**, soft drop shadow (blur ~8 px, 40% black), sentence case | ref10, ref11, ref08 |
| Size (1080 wide) | 64–76 px base | ref10, ref07 |
| Words per caption | phrase-level, 2–4 words, change on natural phrase breaks (not word-by-word pop) | ref07, ref10 |
| Position | lower-middle, 62–72 % height, centred | ref10, ref11 |
| Animation | quick fade/slide-up 4–6 frames, no bounce | all |
| Keyword moment (2–4 per video) | **two-tier**: small light/regular line ("to make a") + one **big display word** below it ("STATEMENT") | ref09, ref08, ref11, ref10 |
| Display word font | heavy condensed caps (Anton-like) **or** tall italic serif (Instrument Serif / Playfair italic) | ref11 / ref08 |
| Display word colour | one brand accent only (F&F blue #2878FE or warm gold #F5C518), never rainbow | ref09 yellow, ref11 orange |
| Hook title (first 1–2 s) | huge 1-word display text, can sit behind the subject | ref10 "VIRAL", ref11 "CONTENT", ref07 "In Last 6 MONTHS" |
| Bottom treatment for premium ads | black gradient over bottom 30 %, serif captions on it | ref07 |

### Graphics & motion
- Proof/screenshot panels and logo strips **behind the subject**, top half, edges faded into the video (ref07).
- Speaker cut out **in front of** UI/app screens (ref10).
- Small white flow cards appearing one by one (Landing page → Add to cart → Purchase) (ref07); numbered "typing" input-bar cards on white for list points (ref11).
- Thin hand-drawn line graphics that follow the speaker's gestures, labels in thin sans (ref11).
- Frosted-glass rounded panels tinted with the accent colour (ref09).
- 3D used sparingly as **layered elements** — e.g. green 3D growth arrows flying up behind the speaker on a grid (ref07). No full-screen 3D dioramas in any reference.
- End: CTA in the brand display font, or the video shrinks into a rounded brand-colour frame (PiP) with doodle arrows (ref11).

### Transitions
Mostly **hard cuts**. Accents: white flash (ref07), warm light-leak / colour flash (ref07, ref10 red flash), whip-pan with directional motion blur between list items (ref08), triangle/shape wipe revealing a title (ref11), invert/glitch frame (ref08). No zoom-blur on every change.

### Music
| Ref | Genre | Tempo | Level under voice (talking) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ref10 | dark minimal **808 trap beat** (sub-bass + kick, almost no melody) | ~118 BPM | ~7–8 dB | constant energy |
| ref08 | 808 beat | ~123 BPM | ~4 dB | **music drops to silence** for 0.3–1 s to punch key lines; loud sting on end card |
| ref11 | warm melodic chords + bass | ~108 BPM | ~11 dB | sub-booms on transitions; sting at end |
| ref07 | none (ambient pad only under CTA) | — | — | SFX-driven |
| ref09 | none | — | — | SFX-driven |
**→ For Salinur:** modern, confident, **bass-led** beat (808/lo-fi trap or warm minimal), 100–125 BPM, **8–12 dB under voice** (not 26), short dropouts before punch lines, sting on CTA. Never quirky/pizzicato/"happy ukulele" (Salinur: the v1/v2 music sounded like comedy).

### Sound effects (Salinur: the v2 whoosh was bad)
What the refs actually use (ref09 even names them):
| Purpose | Sound | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Make a statement | deep **cinematic impact** ("Core") — harmonic low chord + sub | ref09 0.4 s |
| Reveal something unexpected | dramatic **sting** with sustained low bass | ref09 3.8 s |
| Build suspense | **tonal riser** (rising pitched harmonics, not noise), cut dead at the drop | ref09 7–9 s |
| Pop-ups / cards | short **pop + click** pair | ref09, ref07 |
| Transition | **glitch** burst, or **sub-boom drop** | ref09, ref11 |
| Money | **cash register / ka-ching** | ref09 |
| Data reveal | rapid ticks | ref07 |
| Logo strip | soft airy swish (short, high-passed) | ref07 |
Density: 1 SFX every 2–4 s at most; many cuts have none. Level: transients up to ~5 dB under voice peaks for impacts, pops ~15 dB under.

### Colour & framing
Warm cinematic grade (orange/amber practicals, ref07/10/11), soft background blur, deep but not crushed blacks; ref09 moody dark with letterbox bars. Speaker framed mid-chest up, centred.
