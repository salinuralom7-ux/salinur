**Captions:** Poppins ExtraBold, white, 5 px black outline + soft shadow, 1–3 words, word-synced, one yellow (#FFD700) highlight per line. Placed at ~70% height (your chin never goes below 56%, so they never cover your face). Spellings: "Dr. Pandey", "F&F" (Frame & Fame).
**Graphics style:** cards are flat icons (Lucide, ISC licence) on a dark navy (#0F172A) panel with white text and a yellow accent. Your voice keeps running over every card.
**Colour:** slight contrast lift and a touch of warmth. The footage is already well lit.
**Audio:** voice cleaned (high-pass, light de-noise, compression). Music −24 dB under voice with ducking. SFX at about −20 dB. Master −14 LUFS.

## Music

## Not in this video (honest note)
- **No 3D and no stock B-roll this time.** This cloud environment can't reach Pexels/Pixabay, Mixamo or Blender downloads, and a rushed 3D shot would look cheap. The animated icon cards above give a visual change every 3–6 s instead. Once network access is sorted, B-roll and 3D come in.
